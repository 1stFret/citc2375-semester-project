This project is going to be a comprehensive database of NFL stadiums. It will aid users in viewing stadiums and teaching them about what type of stadium each NFL team plays in, capacity, location, and other useful facts. This program will allow even non-NFL fans to feel up to speed on all things related to their closest NFL stadiums.
